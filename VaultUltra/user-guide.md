# Family Vault Ultra User Guide

## Getting Started
Family Vault Ultra helps you organize your family's important documents and information in one secure place. When you first open the app, six starter folders are created for you: **Legal, Financial, Medical, IDs, Property,** and **Personal**. Each one comes with a list of suggested items to help you get started — nothing is required, and you can skip anything that doesn't apply to you.

## Folders
Folders are the main way to organize your information.
- **Smart folders** (the original six) come with suggested items and helpful field templates built in.
- **Custom folders** can be created anytime with the **+** button on the Folders screen — useful for anything that doesn't fit the built-in categories.
- **Suggested Items** appear at the top of a smart folder. Tap one to create it (with fields already set up for that type), or swipe left to skip it if it doesn't apply to you.
- **Reorder folders** by tapping **Edit** on the Folders screen and dragging folders into the order you want.
- **Delete a folder** by swiping left on it. Deleting a folder removes the folder itself. Its items are not deleted, but an item that was only in that folder will no longer appear in your folder lists (you can still find it with Search).

## Items
An item is a single thing you're keeping track of — a bank account, a passport, an insurance policy, and so on.
- Tap **+** inside a folder to create a new item. If the folder has specific suggested types (like Passport or Trust Documents), you'll see those as options; otherwise you'll see general-purpose types like Bank Account or Login/Account.
- Choosing a type creates the item and opens it right away, with the right fields already set up for that kind of item (for example, a Bank Account gets fields for the bank name, account number, and routing number).
- **Changed your mind?** If you go back from a brand-new item before entering anything (no title, no field values, and no attachments), it is discarded automatically. If it came from a suggested item, that suggestion returns to the list.
- An item's **type** can't be changed once it's created — this keeps your organization consistent. You can always create a new item if you need a different type.
- Items are grouped by type within each folder, so all your bank accounts, for example, appear together.
- **Delete an item** by swiping left on it in the folder list. You'll be asked to confirm. Deleting an item also deletes its attachments, and if the folder is shared, the item is removed for everyone who has access.

## Custom Fields
- Tap directly into any field's value to edit it — there's no separate edit screen.
- **Sensitive fields** (like account numbers or passwords) are marked with a dot pattern by default. Tap the eye icon at the top of the item to reveal or hide all sensitive fields at once.
- Add your own fields with **Add Field**, choosing a name, a type (text, date, currency, or number), and whether it should be treated as sensitive.
- **Delete a field** by swiping left on it.

## Attachments
Each item can hold photos, scanned documents, or files.
- **Add Photo** — attach an existing photo from your library.
- **Scan Document** — use your camera to scan a physical document. Family Vault Ultra automatically detects the edges and corrects the perspective for you. The first time you scan, iOS asks for permission to use the camera.
- **Attach File** — import a PDF or other document directly from the Files app or iCloud Drive.
- Tap any attachment to view it. Photos and scans open in a zoomable image viewer; PDFs and other documents open in a full document viewer.
- Scanned and photographed text is automatically recognized in the background, which makes it searchable (see Search, below).

## AI-Assisted Field Extraction
If you've scanned a document with recognizable text, you can swipe left on that attachment and choose **Extract Info** to have key details suggested automatically — for example, pulling a policy number or account name out of a scanned insurance card.
This feature is optional and requires you to add your own Anthropic API key in **Settings**. When you use it, the recognized text from that document is sent to Anthropic for processing. Suggested fields are always shown to you for review first — nothing is saved until you confirm which ones to keep.

## Search
Use the **Search** tab to find anything across your vault — item titles, notes, and text recognized from scanned documents are all searched together.

## Sharing a Folder
Tap the person icon next to any folder in the Folders list to invite someone to that folder — a family member, for example.
1. Add the people you want to invite.
2. Send them the invitation link by Messages or Mail, or copy the link and send it yourself.

Adding someone does not notify them. They receive the invitation when you send the link. They'll need Family Vault Ultra installed, and they must open the link while signed in to the same Apple ID you invited. Only the people you invite can open a shared folder.
Once a folder is shared, tapping the same icon shows you who has access and lets you manage or remove participants.
**If a folder is shared with you:** tap the link they sent. Family Vault Ultra opens, and the shared folder appears in your Folders list.

## Security
- Family Vault Ultra is protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode every time you open it, and it locks again automatically when you leave the app. If Face ID or Touch ID doesn't recognize you, use the passcode option on the prompt.
- Your information is stored in your own private iCloud account and is encrypted. Family Vault Ultra has no servers of its own and cannot access your data. Folders you share are visible to the people you invite.
